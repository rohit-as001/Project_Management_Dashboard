# Project_Management_Dashboard
### This Repository has Project_ Management_ Dashboard projects.


_"Plan Smarter, Deliver Better — Where Project Data Meets Strategic Decision-Making"_


## Project Overview
Project Management Analytics Dashboard is a comprehensive multi-view analytics project built entirely in Microsoft Excel and Power Bi, analyzing the performance of 99 projects across 5 departments, 7 project managers, 4 regions, and a 5-year timeline spanning 2021 to 2025. The project tracks financial returns, project status, complexity distribution, execution efficiency, and phase-level completion rates.
The workbook features two fully interactive dashboard views — an Analytics Dashboard for operational tracking and a Key Insights Dashboard that delivers executive-level strategic narrative — both powered by a Department slicer for dynamic departmental drill-down. A unique AI-style prompt button ("Generate Project Management Report View") adds a modern, intelligent reporting layer to the interface.
This type of analysis is directly applicable to real-world business challenges in PMO (Project Management Office) reporting, portfolio performance reviews, resource allocation optimization, ROI-driven investment planning, and executive decision support.


## Business Objectives
  - Monitor overall project financial performance
  - Track project completion efficiency
  - Identify high-risk or underperforming projects
  - Analyze project distribution by complexity and status
  - Improve strategic planning and portfolio optimization
  - Support leadership with actionable operational insights

  - Total Project Benefit: $874 Million

# Key Insights
1) Exceptional ROI — Benefits More Than Double the Cost
  - Across all 99 projects, the portfolio generated $874M in total benefits against $412M in costs — a net benefit of $462M and an ROI of approximately 112%. This means for every dollar invested, the organization returned over two dollars in benefit. This is a strong, healthy portfolio-level return and validates the overall project selection strategy.


2) 2022 Was the Peak Year — and the Turning Point
  - The year-on-year cost and benefit chart shows 2022 as the clear peak year for both project cost (~$114M) and project benefit (~$237M). Both metrics declined from 2023 onward, stabilizing in 2024–2025. This indicates that the most ambitious and financially productive project cohort was launched in 2022 — and understanding what made 2022 exceptional is critical for replicating that performance.


3) High Cancellation Rate — 27 Projects Cancelled (27%)
  - Out of 99 total projects, 27 were cancelled — the largest single status group other than Completed (30). This is a significant red flag. A 27% cancellation rate suggests systemic issues with project feasibility assessment, resource allocation, scope definition, or stakeholder alignment at the project initiation stage. Every cancelled project represents sunk costs without realized benefits.


4) Strong Completion Rate at 87.01%
  - The average completion percentage across all active and completed projects is 87.01% — demonstrating high operational discipline among project teams. Phase 3 – Plan has the highest completion rate at 91.8%, while Phase 4 – Implement is the weakest at 83.4%, suggesting that execution and implementation phases are where projects most commonly stall.


5) 17 Projects Are On-Hold — A Stalled Pipeline
  - 17 projects (17%) are currently on-hold — representing resources that are committed but not delivering value. On-hold projects create team ambiguity, inflate WIP (Work In Progress), and delay benefit realization. These need to be either decisively restarted or formally closed to free capacity.


6) Five Departments — Differentiated Performance Potential
  - The dashboard supports department-level filtering across: Admin & BI, eCommerce, Sales and Marketing, Supply Chain, and Warehouse. Each department manages a unique mix of project types, complexity levels, and PM assignments. Department-level performance variance is a key analytical lever for targeted improvement interventions.


7) Four-Region Coverage — Geographic Execution Distribution
  - Projects span North, South, East, and West regions, allowing regional performance analysis. Differences in completion rates, cancellation rates, and benefit realization across regions could reveal geographic resource gaps, management quality differences, or regional market conditions affecting project outcomes.



# Business Recommendations
1) Improve Project Selection Process - (High cancellation rates suggest weak initial evaluation.)
  - Strengthen feasibility analysis
  - Introduce risk-scoring models
  - Improve stakeholder requirement validation


2) Focus on Implementation Efficiency - (Implementation phase has the lowest completion performance.)
  - Increase monitoring during execution
  - Improve communication between teams
  - Use milestone-based tracking


3) Replicate 2022 Success Factors - (2022 delivered the best financial outcomes.)
  - Analyze:
    - Team structure
    - Budget allocation
    - Leadership approach
    - Market conditions
  - Replicate successful operational strategies


4) Resolve All 17 On-Hold Projects Within One Quarter
  -On-hold projects consume budget headroom and team attention without delivering value. Management should conduct a formal review of all 17 on-hold projects with a binary decision: restart with revised scope/resources or formally close. This will free capacity, reduce WIP complexity, and improve portfolio clarity.


5) Establish a Formal PMO with Standardized Governance
  - The scale of this portfolio — 99 projects across 5 departments, 4 regions, and 5 years — demands formalized PMO governance. Management should create or strengthen a central PMO function with standardized templates, phase-gate reviews, escalation paths, and a real-time portfolio dashboard (potentially upgrading from Excel to Power BI) to improve visibility and decision speed.
